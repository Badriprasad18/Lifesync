## 1. Executive Summary & Non-Medical Ethical Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Safety & Regulatory Boundaries:**
> The LifeSync AI Personalized Wellness Recommendation Engine provides **educational lifestyle habit optimization and supportive behavioral suggestions only**.
> - **The engine does NOT generate medical prescriptions, pharmacotherapy advice, or psychiatric treatment plans.**
> - **The engine strictly avoids extreme diets (e.g. prolonged starvation, crash diets, severe caloric restriction) and dangerous, high-intensity exercise regimes.**
> - Recommendations use supportive, encouraging, and non-judgmental language designed to foster sustainable micro-habits rather than punitive judgments.

## 3. Seven Standard Output Attributes

Every recommendation generated by the engine strictly adheres to the 7 required fields:

| Field Name | Type | Description | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`id`** | Integer | Unique identifier for tracking and state management | `1` |
| **`category`** | String | One of the 6 core wellness pillars | `"Sleep"` |
| **`observation`** / **`title`** | String | Specific empirical habit observation derived from user input | `"Severe Sleep Duration Restriction"` |
| **`action_item`** / **`suggested_action`** | String | Realistic, achievable, age-appropriate micro-habit | `"Set a non-negotiable bedtime tonight and prioritize a minimum 7.5-hour sleep window."` |
| **`reason`** / **`description`** | String | Physiological or behavioral rationale explaining *why* | `"Sustained sleep restriction below 6 hours diminishes prefrontal cortex executive control and immune function."` |
| **`priority`** | String | Triage level: `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `positive` | `"high"` |
| **`created_at`** | DateTime | ISO-8601 UTC timestamp of recommendation formulation | `"2026-11-01T17:30:00Z"` |

## 4. The Six Lifestyle Categories & Behavioral Rules

### 4.1 Sleep
- **High Priority Trigger:** `sleep_hours < 5.5` or `sleep_quality <= 2`.
  - *Observation:* Severe Sleep Duration Restriction.
  - *Action:* Establish an anchored sleep schedule targeting a minimum 7.5-hour uninterrupted opportunity window.
  - *Reason:* Extended sleep restriction degrades cognitive processing speed, blunts emotional regulation, and increases metabolic strain.
- **Medium Priority Triggers:** `sleep_hours < 6.5`, `sleep_consistency <= 2`, or `screen_before_bed == True`.
  - *Action:* Anchor morning wake time within a 30-minute window and implement a 45-minute digital curfew before sleep.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `sleep_hours >= 7.5`, `sleep_quality >= 4`, `sleep_consistency >= 4`.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Exemplary Restorative Sleep Consistency.

### 4.2 Physical Activity
- **High Priority Trigger:** `sitting_hours >= 9.5` and `exercise_minutes < 25`.
  - *Observation:* Extended Daily Sedentary Pattern.
  - *Action:* Introduce 5 minutes of gentle walking or standing mobility stretches for every 50 minutes of desk work.
  - *Reason:* Prolonged sitting reduces muscular lipoprotein lipase activity and increases cervical and lumbar tension.
- **Medium Priority Trigger:** `activity_frequency_days <= 1`.
  - *Action:* Schedule 25–30 minutes of moderate, enjoyable movement (brisk walking, cycling, or recreational sports) 3 times weekly.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `exercise_minutes >= 35` and `activity_frequency_days >= 3`.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Consistent Movement & Fitness Cadence.

### 4.3 Hydration
- **High Priority Triggers:** `water_intake_liters < 1.4` OR `caffeine_cups >= 3 and water_intake_liters < 2.0`.
  - *Observation:* Marked Daily Hydration Deficit / High Caffeine-to-Hydration Ratio.
  - *Action:* Drink a 350ml glass of water upon waking, keep a refillable 1-liter bottle at your desk, and pair every coffee with equal pure water.
  - *Reason:* Mild dehydration impairs cellular fluid balance, reduces blood plasma volume, and triggers afternoon headaches.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `water_intake_liters >= 2.5`.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Optimal Daily Hydration Practice.

### 4.4 Nutrition
- **High Priority Trigger:** `meals_regularity <= 1` (frequently skipping meals).
  - *Observation:* Irregular Meal Timing & Skips.
  - *Action:* Establish predictable meal windows and avoid skipping midday meals during demanding study or work periods.
  - *Reason:* Irregular meal cadence provokes rapid glycemic swings and reactive cortisol release, triggering afternoon fatigue.
- **Medium Priority Trigger:** `fruit_veg_servings < 2.0`.
  - *Action:* Add one serving of colorful fresh fruit to breakfast and a vegetable portion to lunch or dinner.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `fruit_veg_servings >= 4.0` and `meals_regularity >= 2`.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Diverse, Nutrient-Dense Food Choices.

### 4.5 Mental Wellness
- **High Priority Trigger:** `stress_level >= 7/10` or Anomaly Detection flag.
  - *Observation:* Elevated Perceived Stress & Tension.
  - *Action:* Practice 5 minutes of diaphragmatic box breathing (4s inhale, 4s hold, 4s exhale, 4s hold) and set a clear evening work cutoff.
  - *Reason:* Paced slow respiration stimulates the vagus nerve, calming sympathetic nervous system arousal and reducing physiological tension.
- **Medium Priority Trigger:** `work_life_balance <= 2`.
  - *Action:* Block out 30 minutes of daily restorative offline personal downtime.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `stress_level <= 3` and `work_life_balance >= 4`.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Resilient Mental Wellness & Balanced Perspective.

### 4.6 Digital Habits
- **High Priority Trigger:** Total screen time $\ge 11.0$ hours or `screen_break_frequency == 'rarely'`.
  - *Observation:* High Continuous Screen Exposure.
  - *Action:* Adopt the 20-20-20 visual rest guideline (every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds).
  - *Reason:* Sustained near-distance visual convergence strains ciliary muscles and reduces spontaneous blinking by up to 60%.
- **Positive Habit Recognition:** `recreational_screen_hours <= 2.0` with regular breaks.
  - *Acknowledgment:* Mindful Digital Boundaries & Screen Breaks.

## 5. Machine Learning Pattern & Longitudinal Trend Integration

### 5.1 Machine Learning Contextualization
- **Cluster Archetype Sensitivity:**
  - *Stressed Academic / Crammer:* Contextualizes advice around avoiding all-nighters, taking scheduled Pomodoro breaks, and avoiding midday sugar crashes.
  - *Sedentary Digital Workaholic:* Contextualizes advice around postural reset stretches, standing breaks, and blue-light moderation.
- **Isolation Forest Multivariate Anomaly Integration:**
  - Detects extreme lifestyle combinations (e.g., sitting $>13$ hours with screen $>14$ hours and sleep $<4$ hours).
  - Formulates high-priority stabilizing advice highlighting the specific statistical outlier factors.

### 5.2 Longitudinal Trend Tracking & Progress Celebration
When previous assessment records exist:
- **Sleep Improvement:** Acknowledges increases in sleep duration (e.g. *"Positive Sleep Progress: +1.5 hrs since your last assessment"*).
- **Hydration Growth:** Acknowledges increases in fluid intake (e.g. *"Hydration Habit Growth: +1.0 L since your prior assessment"*).
- **Stagnation Prevention:** If a habit deficit has been flagged in consecutive assessments, updates the suggested action to a progressive, achievable micro-step rather than repeating stale text.

## 6. REST API Endpoints Specification

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`GET`** | `/api/recommendations/` | Retrieve user's recommendations (supports query filters `category`, `priority`, `is_completed`, and `latest_only`). |
| **`GET`** | `/api/recommendations/summary` | Retrieve aggregated dashboard counts (active, completed, positive habits, category & priority breakdown). |
| **`GET`** | `/api/recommendations/assessment/{id}` | Retrieve recommendations for a specific assessment session. |
| **`PATCH`** | `/api/recommendations/{id}/toggle` | Toggle `is_completed` status (`True` $\leftrightarrow$ `False`) for user habit tracking. |
